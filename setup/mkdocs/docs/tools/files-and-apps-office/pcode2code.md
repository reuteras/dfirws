**File Extensions:** `.doc`, `.xls`, `.ppt`

**Tags:** office, vba, decompiler

A vba p-code decompiler based on pcodedmp

## Tips
Decompiles VBA p-code, recovering macros whose source was stomped (VBA stomping). Compare with olevba output to detect stomping.

## Usage
pcode2code <file.doc>
