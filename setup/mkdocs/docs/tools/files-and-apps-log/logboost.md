Convert a variety of log formats to CSV while enriching detected IPs with Geolocation, ASN, DNS, WhoIs, Shodan InternetDB and Threat Indicator matches.

## Tips
Converts many log formats to CSV and enriches IPs with GeoIP, ASN, DNS, WhoIs and threat intel. Only downloaded with -LogBoost; the enrichment databases come from the enrichment data set.

## Usage
dfirws-install.ps1 -LogBoost
